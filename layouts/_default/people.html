Teng Zhang
Associate Professor, Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering

Teng Zhang is an Associate Professor in the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at Syracuse University. His research uses mechanics to understand and design materials and structures, drawing on problems from biology, soft matter, and engineering. He did his Ph.D. at Brown University with Huajian Gao, and was a postdoctoral fellow at MIT before joining Syracuse in 2016.

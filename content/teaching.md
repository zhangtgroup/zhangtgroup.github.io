# Teaching & Outreach

## Courses at Syracuse University

- MAE 635, Advanced Mechanics of Materials (graduate)
- ECS 325, Mechanics of Solids (undergraduate)
- MAE 600, Bioinspired Communication and Ethics (graduate)
- MAE 682, Computational Solid Mechanics (graduate, 2016–2021)

## Outreach

The group hosts high school summer interns and undergraduate researchers through programs such as LSAMP and REU, and brings hands-on mechanics demonstrations to local communities.
